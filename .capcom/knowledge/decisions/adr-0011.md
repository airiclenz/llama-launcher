---
title: A curated public facade makes the launcher importable as a library
---

Status: Accepted; amended 2026-09-24.

The public package `launcher/` is a minimal facade of type aliases and thin wrappers over the
unchanged `internal/launcher/`, and its documented surface is the SemVer contract. Read
`adr-0011-decision` for the exported surface and the four contract decisions, then
`adr-0011-why` for the first client and the rejected shapes, then `adr-0011-consequences`, then
`adr-0011-amend-0924` for `Stop` as the supported cancellation of a waiting `LoadProfile`.
