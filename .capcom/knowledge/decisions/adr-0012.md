---
title: The library compiles everywhere and actuates where it can
---

Status: Accepted; amended 2026-07-29 and 2026-09-24.

The library compiles on darwin, linux and windows; every verb works where its mechanism exists,
and where it does not it returns the exported sentinel `ErrUnsupported`. Read
`adr-0012-decision` for the per-OS contract, then `adr-0012-rest` for the rejected alternatives,
the `ErrStartupTimeout` sentinel and the consequences, then the amendments in date order:
`adr-0012-amend-0729`, `adr-0012-amend-0924` and `adr-0012-amend-windows`.
