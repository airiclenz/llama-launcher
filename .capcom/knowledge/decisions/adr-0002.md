---
title: llama-launcher is a process manager, not a request router
---

Status: Accepted.

The launcher starts and stops LLM Servers and loads and unloads Models; it exposes no endpoint and
never routes client requests. Read `adr-0002-decision` for the rule, then `adr-0002-why` for the
two meanings of routing and why the launcher does neither, then `adr-0002-consequences` for what
clients see.
