---
title: llamacpp uses restart-per-Profile, not multi-Model hosting
---

Status: Accepted.

Activating a `llamacpp` Profile restarts `llama-server` with the Profile's Model and hardware flags
instead of loading Models through its HTTP API. Read `adr-0003-decision` for the rule, then
`adr-0003-why` for the upstream bug that forces it, then `adr-0003-consequences`, then
`adr-0003-revisit` for when to weigh multi-Model hosting again.
