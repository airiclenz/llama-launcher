---
title: A loading Splash is found by its process, not by its address
---

Status: Accepted; amended 2026-09-24.

A loading Splash, which has not bound its address yet, is found in the process table by its
command line, and only while nothing listens on the address; this amends ADR-0010 for Splash.
Read `adr-0015-decision` for the match and where it is used, then `adr-0015-why` for the
rejected launch record, then `adr-0015-consequences`, which carries a dated amendment on argv
reading.
